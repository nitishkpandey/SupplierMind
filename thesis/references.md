# References

References are listed alphabetically by author surname in the Harvard (Cite Them
Right) style. For works with more than three authors, the first author is given
followed by *et al.*, as is standard in this style. Every entry corresponds to an
in-text citation in the preceding chapters. All scholarly references were verified
against the DBLP computer-science bibliography, and where a work exists both as a
preprint and as a peer-reviewed publication, the peer-reviewed venue is cited.
